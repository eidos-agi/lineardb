# Code of Conduct

LinearDB follows the Eidos AGI expectation for professional, evidence-first
collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Argue from evidence and tests.
- Keep private data private.
- Make risks and uncertainty explicit.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets, private customer data, or personal data.
- Bypassing credential, approval, or outbound-communication boundaries.

Report concerns to the project maintainers through the repository issue or
security contact listed in `SECURITY.md`.
